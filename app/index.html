    Standing rules

      IEC 62923-2

      Alert identifier ranges

      Unlisted does not mean optional. It means manufacturer-defined, outside Annex A, and it must still be documented.

      Range Role Notes
      0001–0099 Emergency alarms Reserved for emergency use only
      0100–0299 Reserved Future expansion
      0300–9999 Standard / graded alerts Use Annex A IDs when the alert is listed
      10000–9999999 Unlisted / manufacturer-defined Maker must publish the full ID catalogue for the equipment

      What to demand from the maker

      • Complete list of all alerts in the equipment with assigned identifiers
      • Plain-language meaning, priority/category, and recommended bridge response
      • Confirmation that Annex A alerts use standard IDs, and all others sit in 10000–9999999

      Shipboard quick checks

      • If the ID is ≥ 10000, open that equipment’s BAM catalogue before guessing
      • If AMS text has no alarm-list row, treat configuration/documentation as part of the defect
      • Keep a local quick-reference of proven unlisted IDs for the next watch
